commonMistakes:
  - wrong: Je suis trente ans
    right: J'ai trente ans
    why: Age uses "avoir" (to have years) in French, never "être" (to be).
  - wrong: Quatre-vingts
    right: quatre-vingt (before another number)
    why: 'The -s drops when another number follows: quatre-vingt-cinq (85).'
  - wrong: Dix-sept without the hyphen
    right: dix-sept
    why: '17, 18 and 19 are written hyphenated — dix-sept, dix-huit, dix-neuf.'

# Numbers and basic questions

## Numbers 0–20

| | | | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 zéro | 1 un | 2 deux | 3 trois |
| 4 quatre | 5 cinq | 6 six | 7 sept |
| 8 huit | 9 neuf | 10 dix | 11 onze |
| 12 douze | 13 treize | 14 quatorze | 15 quinze |
| 16 seize | 17 dix-sept | 18 dix-huit | 19 dix-neuf |
| 20 vingt | | | |

Listen once, then shadow: point at things and count them — *une chaise,
deux chaises, trois chaises*. Numbers above 20 follow a pattern
(21 = vingt et un, 22 = vingt-deux...) — you will meet them next.

**Pronunciation traps:** *cinq* ends with a nasal "k" sound; *six* = "see";
*sept* = "set" (final t silent); *neuf* = "nuhf".

## Asking and telling age

- **Quel âge as-tu ?** / **Quel âge avez-vous ?** — How old are you?
- **J'ai dix ans.** — I am ten (literally *I have ten years*).

The verb is **avoir** (to have). "Je suis dix ans" is a classic beginner
error — avoid it.

## The six question words

| French | English | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qui | who | Qui est-ce ? |
| quoi / que | what | Tu veux quoi ? |
| où | where | Où est la gare ? |
| quand | when | Quand part le train ? |
| comment | how | Comment ça va ? |
| pourquoi | why | Pourquoi ? |

A question can be as simple as one word with rising intonation:
**Où ?** — *Where?* You already know how to build full questions from
earlier lessons; these words let you ask about anything.

## Practice drill

Cover the table and write 0–20 from memory. Then answer aloud:
*Quel âge as-tu ?* — use your real age or invent one; the pattern
**J'ai ... ans** is what matters.
