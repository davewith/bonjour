# Shopping and prices

Markets and shops are where your numbers finally pay off. Two frames —
asking the price and stating it — plus one buying phrase handle almost
every purchase you will make in France.

## Asking the price

- **Ça coûte combien ?** — How much does it cost? (the everyday form)
- **C'est à quel prix ?** — What is the price? (fuller, less common)
- **C'est combien ?** — spoken shortcut, fine with friends

Point at the item and say **Ça coûte combien ?** — nothing else needed.
In a market, add the noun: **Ça coûte combien, ces tomates ?**

## Saying what it costs

**Ça coûte + number + euros**:

- Ça coûte dix euros. — It costs ten euros.
- Ça coûte vingt-cinq euros. — It costs twenty-five euros.
- C'est cher. / C'est bon marché. — It's expensive. / It's cheap.

The verb is **coûter** and its subject is **ça** — never "c'est coûtre".

## Numbers 20–100 quick reference

| Number | French | Number | French |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | vingt | 60 | soixante |
| 21 | vingt et un | 70 | soixante-dix |
| 30 | trente | 80 | quatre-vingts |
| 40 | quarante | 90 | quatre-vingt-dix |
| 50 | cinquante | 100 | cent |

Two traps to remember: 80 is **quatre-vingts** (four-twenties), but the
-s drops before another number — quatre-vingt-cinq = 85. And 70, 80, 90
are counted in tens-and-ones — soixante-dix (60-10), quatre-vingt-dix
(80-10). Practice the tens aloud until they stick.

## Buying politely

1. Ask — **Ça coûte combien ?**
2. Decide — **Je prends la chemise, s'il vous plaît.**
3. Wrong fit? — **C'est trop grand** / **C'est trop petit.**
4. Pay and leave — **Voilà. Merci, bonne journée !**

"Je veux" (I want) is grammatically fine but blunt in shops; "Je
prends" (I'll take) is what buyers actually say.

## A price dialogue

> — Bonjour, madame. Je cherche des chaussures.
> — Voici. Elles sont à vingt-cinq euros.
> — Ça coûte combien les pantalons ?
> — Les pantalons, trente euros.
> — D'accord, je prends les chaussures, s'il vous plaît.
> — Très bien. Ça fait vingt-cinq euros.
> — Voilà. Merci !
> — Je vous en prie, bonne journée !

Play both roles aloud with the audio. Then redo it with different
items and prices — la chemise (trente euros), le pantalon (quarante
euros) — until the frames come out without thinking.
