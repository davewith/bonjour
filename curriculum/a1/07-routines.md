# Talking about everyday routines

Routines are the first thing beginners can say in full — the vocabulary
is small, the verbs are regular, and you can describe your whole morning
with six or seven sentences.

## -ER verbs in the present tense

| | parler | manger | travailler |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **je** | je parle | je mange | je travaille |
| **tu** | tu parles | tu manges | tu travailles |
| **il** | il parle | il mange | il travaille |

One pattern: remove **-er**, then add **-e** (je), **-es** (tu),
**-e** (il). For *je* and *il* the spelling matches — only the pronoun
changes. In *manger* the "g" keeps its soft sound before e (je mange =
"monzh"), and the final -s of *tu manges* is silent.

## Time words

| French | English |
| --- | --- |
| le matin | in the morning |
| le soir | in the evening |
| à sept heures | at seven o'clock |
| chaque jour / tous les jours | every day |
| d'abord | first |
| puis | then |
| enfin | finally |

Clock times always take **à** — "à sept heures". Never "en sept heures".

## Routine vocabulary

| French | English |
| --- | --- |
| se lever — je me lève | to get up |
| prendre le petit-déjeuner — je prends le petit-déjeuner | to have breakfast |
| manger — je mange | to eat |
| travailler — je travaille | to work |
| étudier — j'étudie | to study |
| dormir — je dors | to sleep (irregular — learn it as a chunk) |

Note **j'étudie**: "je" becomes "j'" before a vowel, exactly like
"j'habite".

## Mini-dialogue about mornings

> — À quelle heure te lèves-tu ?
> — Je me lève à sept heures, et toi ?
> — Moi, je me lève à six heures. D'abord je prends le petit-déjeuner,
>   puis je travaille.
> — Tu étudies le soir ?
> — Oui, j'étudie le soir, puis je dors à minuit.

Practise both roles aloud. Replace the times and activities with your own
routine until you can say it without looking.

## Pronunciation targets

- **je travaille** — two syllables, "trah-VYE"; the "aille" glide rhymes
  with English "vie", and the final -e is silent.
- **me lève** — "muh LEV"; stress the verb, keep the final -e silent.
- **à sept heures** — "ah set UHR"; the h in heures is silent and the two
  words link together.
- **d'abord** — "dah-BOR"; the final d is silent.
- **je dors** — "zhuh DOR"; the -s of dormir is silent in this form.

Play the spoken examples and shadow each line — once slowly, once at
natural speed.
